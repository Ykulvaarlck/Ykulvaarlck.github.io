How it works

Width and height set how big the picture is drawn, not how many pixels it has: they change the text size and leave the resolution alone. "Pixels per stud"/"Downsample factor" let you downsample the image, and "Reset" puts it back at full resolution.

Pixels get turned into text components on bricks, packed on the left side of the prefab. They are placed approximately where the pixels themselves lie in-world. When the selected size is not a multiple of micro in size, the image slightly extends outside the prefab on the bottom and right sides. An invisible red brick is added as padding on the right, to make the prefab's size match the image's.

Lowering the quality merges adjacent pixels' colors, using fewer color tags and allowing you to save on bricks. The PELT algorithm is used to pick the best-looking way to do this, depending on the quality.
